## Tips

- Turn the renders into **Reels** too: a short video of the printer making a keychain (plus the colour change) gets far more views than a photo.
- Once you have real prints, post **real photos next to these images** (on a hand, on keys, on a bag): customers trust real photos more.
- Story ideas: a "Quel prénom pour le prochain ?" poll, client reviews, "behind the scenes" of the printer.
